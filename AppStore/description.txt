Bed Ledger helps home growers remember what they planted, where, and when. Create named beds with optional dimensions and notes. Record seasons, crop plantings, and harvest dates and amounts. Before planting again, browse each bed's history across seasons.

Made for Windows, Android phones and tablets, and iPhone/iPad. Your journal stays on your device. No account, ads, analytics, or location permission. Export a JSON backup or CSV harvest log when you choose; preview a JSON backup before importing it.

Bed Ledger is a personal record, not a source of plant identification, gardening advice, yield predictions, or food-safety guidance.
