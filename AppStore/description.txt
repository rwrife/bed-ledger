Bed Ledger helps home growers remember what they planted, where, and when. Sketch your growing space as named beds, keep a season-by-season planting journal, and record harvest dates and amounts. Before the next planting, look back at a bed's own history instead of guessing.

Planned for Windows, Android phones and tablets, and iPhone/iPad. Data stays on your device. No account, ads, or location permission. Export a JSON backup or CSV harvest log when you choose.

Bed Ledger is a personal record, not a source of plant identification, gardening advice, yield predictions or food-safety guidance.
